Search evaluation

Label the ranked results for a small, fixed query set. Labels are saved in this browser and can be exported as JSON for evaluation.

Relevant: The painting is a direct match or is meaningfully similar in subject, appearance, style, title, artist, or other semantic connection.
Unsure: The painting may be similar, but the connection is weak, ambiguous, or depends heavily on interpretation.
Not relevant: The painting is a clear miss and would not be useful for that search.
* For exact title-queries: mark the exact painting, paintings with similar titles, and visually or semantically similar paintings as relevant.
