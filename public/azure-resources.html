Art Explorer: Azure resources and costs

Resource What it does Cost
Container App Runs the website and search models. Consumption plan, 2 CPUs / 4 GiB RAM: pay-as-you-go, not the smallest size. About $47/month for one idle replica. Stays warm between visits; active use may cost more. Estimate before subscription free grants.
Container Apps environment Hosts the app's runtime and networking. Basic Consumption setup: no dedicated compute. No separate base compute fee.
PostgreSQL B1ms Stores artworks and embeddings; finds matching images. B1ms Burstable: smallest compute size. 32 GiB: minimum storage. About $16/month. Stays on; no automatic scale-to-zero.
Azure Files and storage account Keeps model files so wake-ups do not require downloading them again. Standard LRS: basic, locally redundant storage. Small usage-based storage and access charges, including while asleep.
Container Registry, Basic Stores the packaged application. Basic: lowest paid registry tier. About $5/month. Charged even while the app sleeps.
Log Analytics Saves logs for troubleshooting. Pay-as-you-go: no fixed tier. Daily ingestion cap configured. Usage-based; depends on log volume.
Managed identity and role assignment Gives the app permission to download its package. No paid tier. No separate charge.
Resource group Groups the resources together. No paid tier. Free.
Total estimate Our always-on app setup. West US 3 estimates, October 2026, before free grants. About $68/month baseline + storage, logs and active app usage. Your $150 credit is shared with other Azure projects.
